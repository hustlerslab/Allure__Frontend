# Glossary

## Purpose

This glossary defines the terminology used throughout Layer 2 (Guided Journey). It provides a common vocabulary for developers, architects, testers, and stakeholders.

---

# A

## API

Application Programming Interface used for communication between Layer 2 and external services.

---

## AI Analysis

The process of analyzing customer requirements using Layer 3 AI services to generate recommendations.

---

## Approval

The business process where quotations or project plans are accepted before execution.

---

# B

## Business Rules

Deterministic rules that govern workflow execution. Business rules always take precedence over AI recommendations.

---

# C

## Checkpoint

A saved workflow state that allows the journey to resume after failures or interruptions.

---

## Correlation ID

A unique identifier attached to requests and events for end-to-end traceability.

---

# D

## Designer Matching

The process of selecting suitable designers based on project requirements and AI recommendations.

---

## Discovery

The initial stage of the Guided Journey where users explore ideas, templates, and inspirations.

---

# E

## Event

A notification representing a completed business action that can trigger downstream processing.

---

## Event Bus

Infrastructure responsible for publishing and consuming events between services.

---

## Execution

The phase where approved projects move into implementation.

---

# G

## Guided Journey

The end-to-end workflow managed by Layer 2, from discovery through project completion.

---

# H

## Handover

The final stage where the completed project is delivered to the customer.

---

# I

## Idempotency

A property ensuring that repeated execution of the same request produces the same result without duplicate side effects.

---

# J

## Journey

A complete customer workflow managed by the Journey Orchestrator.

---

## Journey Orchestrator

The central component responsible for coordinating workflow execution across Layer 2.

---

# K

## Kafka

An event streaming platform used for asynchronous communication between services.

---

# L

## Layer 2

The Guided Journey layer responsible for orchestrating customer workflows.

---

# O

## Observability

The ability to monitor system behavior through logs, metrics, and distributed tracing.

---

# P

## Payment

The workflow responsible for processing customer payments before project execution.

---

## Payment Gateway

An external service responsible for authorizing and processing financial transactions.

---

# Q

## Quotation

A cost estimate generated for the customer before approval.

---

# R

## Retry

The process of reattempting an operation after a temporary failure.

---

## Redis

An in-memory data store used for caching and temporary data.

---

# S

## Saga Pattern

A distributed transaction management pattern using a sequence of local transactions and compensation actions.

---

## State Machine

A workflow model that defines valid state transitions throughout the Guided Journey.

---

## Service

An independently deployable software component providing specific business capabilities.

---

# T

## Template

A predefined design or project blueprint that customers can customize.

---

## Trust Verification

The process of validating customer or provider trust information using Layer 5 services.

---

# U

## User Journey

The sequence of interactions a customer performs while using the platform.

---

# W

## Workflow

An ordered sequence of business activities coordinated by the Journey Orchestrator.
